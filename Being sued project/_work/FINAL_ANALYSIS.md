### 2.2 The Trustee's Legal Bedrock (Now Clearer)

The trustee does NOT primarily rely on art. 110 L.F. The real doctrinal framework is:

- **Art. 41 TUB** — the *credito fondiario* creditor retains individual foreclosure even during bankruptcy
- **Art. 52 L.F.** (now CCII art. 151) — but the proceeds of that foreclosure are subject to *concorso formale* of all bankruptcy creditors
- **Art. 111 L.F.** (now CCII art. 221) — prededuzioni and privilegi rank as established in bankruptcy, not enforcement
- **Cass. 23482/2018 and Cass. 12673/2022** — the assegnazione at enforcement is *provvisoria* and the surplus over the *passivo ammesso* must be restituted to the curatela **ex lege**, i.e. by operation of law — not dependent on impugnazione of the distribution plan
- **Cass. SS.UU. 25749/2015** — reinforces the principle that bankruptcy supersedes individual enforcement for ranking purposes
- **Cass. SU 4309/2010** — exclusivity of the accertamento del passivo; admission decrees cannot be collaterally attacked

This means the "finality of 04.04.2019 distribution plan" argument is **not a silver bullet**. The surplus-restitution duty arises independent of whether the plan was challenged.

## OVERALL SUMMARY (ONE-PAGE)

**The trustee's case is legally strong, not weak.** The €1.188m credit argument does not defeat the core *restitution ex lege* principle under Italian bankruptcy law. Most of the defences that feel intuitive to a non-Italian lawyer (clerical error, finality of distribution plan, full credit value) have already been addressed by Cassazione case law against us.

**The realistic best outcome at trial is a modest quantum reduction (perhaps €3-7k), and the worst outcome is the full €57,536.75 plus ~€25-35k in interest and costs. This downside exceeds €85k.**

**The rational strategy is to file a tightly focused defence by 04.05.2026 and immediately open settlement talks targeting a €40-45k landing, with a walkaway ceiling of €52k.**

**Anything beyond this — fighting hard on weak arguments, attacking the €30k refund, chasing Fiorilli in this proceeding — costs money and damages credibility without improving outcomes.**

**Walk back from the €1.188m red herring. It is a red herring.**
