## The €57,536.75 Dispute

### Trustee's theory (Avv. Finetti):
- Delegated judge admitted only €572,438.29 as mortgage claim at passivo
- Naissance received €629,975.04 from foreclosure
- Therefore: €629,975.04 − €572,438.29 = **€57,536.75 excess** must be returned
- Legal basis: Cass. 28.09.2018 no. 23482 and 20.04.2022 no. 12673
- Foreclosure allocation is only *provisional*; final ranking done in bankruptcy per art. 110 L.F.

### Our position (from the facts):
The trustee's calculation conflates two different things:
- **€572,438.29** is NOT the full credit - it's explicitly the **residual** after deducting the €616,000 auction assignment from the full €1,188,438.29 credit
- Math proof: €1,188,438.29 − €616,000.00 = €572,438.29 ✓
- Plus €4,761.47 prededuzione legal fees = €577,199.76 (the amount admitted)

### Key text from Fiorilli's 2018 istanza (§4):
> "Euro 572.438,29 (1.188.438,29 sottratta la somma di assegnazione pari ad euro 616.000,00) con collocazione ipotecaria"

**The parenthetical EXPLICITLY STATES** that €572,438.29 = €1,188,438.29 minus €616,000 assignment. This is not a clerical error - it is deliberate residual accounting.

## CRITICAL STRATEGIC INSIGHT

**The trustee is NOT disputing the €1,188,438.29 credit value.** They concede the credit chain in their own pleadings (Banca CRAS → Argo → Naissance). Their argument is narrower and more dangerous:

> "In bankruptcy, only the amount ADMITTED AT PASSIVO (€572,438.29 as mortgage claim) matters. The foreclosure allocation is only *provisional* per Cass. 23482/2018 and 12673/2022. Final ranking is done in bankruptcy per art. 110 L.F. Any excess from foreclosure must be returned."

This means proving the €1.188m credit value — even with documents — does NOT directly defeat the claim. We need different angles.

## Real Defence Angles (Provisional Analysis)

### Strong
- **A: Finality of 04.04.2019 distribution plan** — trustee claims it was "provisional" but if Naissance can show it was definitively approved without reservation and not challenged within the legal time limit, the trustee's entire premise collapses
- **B: €4,761.47 prededuzione should have been admitted** — if the G.D. wrongly excluded this, the admitted amount should be €577,199.76 not €572,438.29, reducing the gap to €52,775.28
- **C: €3,932.00 legal fees "not admitted to bankruptcy liabilities"** — if these were legitimately distributed in foreclosure, they're not clawback-able even on trustee's own theory
- **D: Set-off of procedural costs Naissance actually advanced** — if Naissance paid procedural costs not yet refunded, these should be netted against any claim

### Weaker
- **E: "Clerical error" framing** (Belardi's current proposal) — weak because the istanza math is internally consistent (€1.188m − €616k = €572k residual)
- **F: Full credit value argument** — trustee doesn't dispute it; art. 110 L.F. framework makes it largely irrelevant
- **G: Limitation periods** — only helps if characterised as bankruptcy clawback action; trustee has carefully pleaded it as simple restitution

### Procedural
- **H: Diffide were sent to Fiorilli's domicile, not Naissance UK directly** — may have notification defects
- **I: Jurisdiction** under art. 3 L.218/95 and Hague Convention 1965 — appears correctly pleaded, hard to attack
- **J: Involve Fiorilli via professional indemnity insurance** — backup only, doesn't help Naissance directly
