## The arithmetic dispute — this is the core of the case

**Fiorilli's framing in the 2018 Istanza (points 3-4):**
- "L'istante vantando un credito di €.1.188.438,29 ... ha chiesto ed ottenuto in sede esecutiva l'assegnazione dell'immobile ... per un importo di €.616.000,00"
- "il credito ... ammonta attualmente a complessivamente Euro 577.199,76 e precisamente: €4.761,47 in prededuzione ... €572.438,29 (1.188.438,29 sottratta la somma di assegnazione pari ad euro 616.000,00) con collocazione ipotecaria"
- **Fiorilli's logic:** Total credit = €1,188,438.29. Enforcement already gave Naissance €616,000. Remaining credit = €572,438.29. Plus €4,761.47 legal fees. Admit for €577,199.76.

**The Giudice Delegato's admission:** Formal admission of €577,199.76 (€572,438.29 privilege + €4,761.47 prededuzione). The admission recites Fiorilli's arithmetic.

**Trustee's theory in the Ricorso:**
- Cites Cass. 23482/2018 and 12673/2022: art. 41 TUB privilege is procedural only; allocation is definitive only through bankruptcy.
- Therefore: €572,438.29 is Naissance's total entitlement from this insolvency (not an "after netting €616k" residual).
- Naissance received €629,975.04 from the enforcement.
- Excess over admitted passivo = €629,975.04 − €572,438.29 = **€57,536.75**.
- Interest from due date under art. 1284(4) c.c.

**The core legal question:**
Did the admission to passivo of €572,438.29 represent (a) Naissance's residual credit after accounting for the €616k already received in enforcement (Fiorilli's view), or (b) Naissance's total entitlement from the insolvency, meaning the €616k already received was an excess to be returned (trustee's view)?

This is the battleground. The Cassazione citations in the Ricorso support (b). The text of the 2018 Istanza itself supports (a).

## Belardi's strategy (from 17.04.2026 email)

**Primary:** Argue that the 2018 Istanza contains a "material error" (errore materiale). The premises of the Istanza state the credit as €1,188,438.29; the Istanza then asks admission for only €577,199.76. Belardi will argue the Giudice should apply common sense (buonsenso) and recognise the full €1.188m credit, which would mean Naissance was under-paid, not over-paid.

**Subordinate:** If the court rejects the "material error" argument, Avv. Fiorilli must handle the matter via his professional liability insurance (as the original drafter of the admittedly ambiguous Istanza).

**Settlement:** Belardi considers settlement with the trustee realistic, on economic terms materially lower than €57,536.75.
